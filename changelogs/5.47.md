# 5.47.0
Released 26th October 2026.

**Recommended PHP binaries: [php-8.4-20261026](https://gitlab.com/VanillaPocketMine/PHP-Binaries/-/releases/php-8.4-20261026)** (chunkutils2 0.3.15). They bring the native code this release uses: native entity physics and pathfinding, SIMD light population, native random ticks, light arrays shared when uniform (about 25-30% less main-thread memory with many chunks loaded) and precise tick timing on Windows. Older 0.3.x binaries keep working, without those speed-ups. Pterodactyl eggs download the latest binaries on reinstall.

This is a feature release for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition 1.26.52 (display version 26.52, protocol 2193): the vanilla behaviour release (mobs and their AI, blocks, items and mechanics), made without slowing the server down. Plugins for previous 5.x versions run unchanged: the plugin API only gained additions.

## Gameplay
- Sulfur cubes absorb blocks: give a medium cube a block (or let it hop to one on the ground) and it takes one of the twelve archetypes of the block's tag (wood bouncy, stone slow bouncy, TNT explosive, magma hot...), shown inside it. With a block it has no AI and becomes a ball players push and kick, with the archetype's bounce, friction, drag and knockback; most damage only knocks it back, it breathes in water and lava, can be leashed and doesn't despawn. Shears take the block out again. TNT cubes are primed by flint and steel, fire charges, fire, magma, burning arrows, redstone and explosions (6 second fuse) and explode with power 3 (`tntexplodes`, blocks only with `mobgriefing`); magma cubes burn whoever touches them. An empty bucket picks a medium cube up into a Bucket of Sulfur Cube, which releases it again. Small cubes are lured by slimeballs and medium ones by blocks; a dead medium cube splits into two small ones. Small cubes are no longer drawn at half size.
- Slimes target and hurt iron and snow golems (magma cubes: iron golems), going for the nearest of them and the players, take fall damage like in Bedrock (magma cubes don't), split in the vanilla positions and play the Bedrock jump and landing sounds. Slimes and magma cubes float in lava as well as water; magma cubes hop out of it higher.
- A leashed axolotl picked up with a water bucket drops its lead, like a sulfur cube picked up with a bucket or exploding.
- Pointed dripstone and sulfur spikes grow (dripstone under a water source, sulfur spikes from sulfur); stalactites that lose their support fall, hurt what they land on (6 per block fallen, or the stalactite's length if longer, at most 40) and shatter; landing on a dripstone stalagmite doubles the fall damage; fast tridents break pointed dripstone.
- Potent sulfur under water gives off noxious gas (Nausea at the surface); over magma it is a geyser that erupts on a countdown, over lava it erupts all the time, pushing mobs and items up. It only updates in ticking chunks.
- Damage follows the Bedrock damage causes: the Wither effect, stalagmites, the Warden's sonic boom and dragon's breath ignore armour (the sonic boom also ignores Protection), thorns damage is reduced by armour, a helmet protects from falling stalactites. Blaze and ghast fireballs are fire damage: Fire Resistance blocks them and Fire Protection reduces them. `/gamerule firedamage false` also protects players from magma blocks, hot sulfur cubes, campfires and fireballs.
- Death messages use the Bedrock texts: dying in fire and burning to death are no longer swapped; starvation, the Wither effect, sweet berry bushes, magma, stalactites, stalagmites, sonic booms, thorns, freezing, magic used by a mob (evoker fangs, thrown Harming potions), fireballs, llama spit, thrown snowballs and eggs, mace smashes, hot sulfur cubes and bed or respawn anchor explosions have their own message; named weapons are named ("using [weapon]"), and dying in fire, lava, magma, water, a cactus or a sonic boom less than 5 seconds after a mob or player hurt you names it ("whilst trying to escape"). Mob names are sent as their vanilla translation key, so each player reads them in the language of their client.
- Mobs and players killed by the Wither (or its skulls, or soon after it hurt them) drop a wither rose.
- Loot follows the Bedrock tables: chests, dispensers, barrels and decorated pots of structures use the Bedrock behaviour pack tables (also the Bedrock-only ones and the BDS names `buriedtreasure`, `shipwreck`, `shipwrecksupply`, `shipwrecktreasure`, `dispenser_trap`); fishing uses the Bedrock fishing tables (jungle fish, treasure weights and enchantments, damaged rods and boots).
- Fire Aspect sets the target on fire before the hit, so a mob killed by the hit drops cooked meat.
- A tamed pet's kill counts as its owner's for experience and for the drops Bedrock gives to kills by players or pets (blaze rods, breeze rods, drowned copper and tridents, zombie iron and vegetables...), without the owner's Looting. Drops that need the player's own kill (spider eyes, phantom membranes, the wither skeleton skull, stray arrows...) still do, and villagers do not blame the owner.
- New worlds can have a bonus chest near the spawn (see below), filled from `chests/spawn_bonus_chest` and surrounded by torches.

## Configuration
- New `bonus-chest` in `server.properties` (default `false`) for the default world, and `bonus-chest: true` in a `worlds` entry of `pocketmine.yml`. Only new overworlds get a chest; Bedrock `level.dat` gets `bonusChestEnabled` and `bonusChestSpawned` (set once the chest is placed, so a server stopped before the spawn chunk was ready places it on the next start).
- New `performance-profile` in `server.properties`: `performance` (default, the behaviour so far: memory and CPU kept ready for a smoother server) or `economy` (as little memory and CPU as possible); `rendimiento` and `ahorro` are accepted too, an unknown value logs a warning and uses `performance`. Both play exactly the same (same gameplay, mobs, terrain and view distance). Economy unloads unused chunks after 10 seconds instead of 30, uses a 4 MB LevelDB memtable per world instead of 16 MB, at most 2 async workers with `async-workers: auto` (instead of 4), 1 chunk generation worker instead of 2, shuts idle async workers down after 60 seconds instead of 300, and runs the vanillagen helper with `GOGC=50`: new chunks can take a little longer to appear. With 100 bots exploring a fresh world, economy used ~15% less memory (process + vanillagen) at the same TPS and CPU; with players standing still in loaded chunks both profiles use the same. The `pocketmine.yml` settings marked `[profile]` follow the profile while they keep their shipped value; any other value overrides the profile. The active profile is logged at startup.

## API
- `pocketmine\entity\animal\SulfurCubeArchetype` (enum; `fromItem()`), `SulfurCube::getArchetype()`, `getAbsorbedBlock()`, `absorbBlock()`, `ejectBlock()`, `prime()`, `isPrimed()`, `getFuse()`, `isFromBucket()`, `split()`; `SulfurCube` implements `BucketableMob` and `Explosive`. `pocketmine\item\SulfurCubeBucket` (`VanillaItems::SULFUR_CUBE_BUCKET()`, a subclass of `UnstackableItem`) with `releaseMob()`. `Slime::MAX_SIZE`; slimes keep sizes up to 127 from saved data. `NearestAttackableEntityGoal` takes an optional `verticalRange` (default 4, as before).
- `PointedDripstone` implements `Fallable`, for falling stalactites only: it never falls when the block below it is removed, and as a `FallingBlock` it shatters into its item instead of landing (a plugin that makes any `Fallable` block fall gets that drop). New sounds `GeyserEruptionStartSound`, `GeyserEruptionActiveSound`, `PointedDripstoneLandSound`.
- New damage causes `EntityDamageEvent::CAUSE_WITHER`, `CAUSE_THORNS`, `CAUSE_MAGMA`, `CAUSE_CAMPFIRE`, `CAUSE_SWEET_BERRY_BUSH`, `CAUSE_STALACTITE`, `CAUSE_STALAGMITE`, `CAUSE_SONIC_BOOM`, `CAUSE_DRAGON_BREATH`, `CAUSE_FIREBALL` and `CAUSE_WITHER_SKULL`, with `EntityDamageEvent::isFireCause()`, `isProjectileCause()` and `canBeReducedByArmorEnchantments()`. These damages had `CAUSE_FIRE` (magma, hot sulfur cubes, campfires), `CAUSE_MAGIC` (Wither effect, thorns, Guardian spikes, sonic boom, dragon's breath), `CAUSE_CONTACT` (sweet berry bushes), `CAUSE_FALLING_BLOCK` (stalactites), `CAUSE_FALL` (stalagmites) or `CAUSE_PROJECTILE` (fireballs, wither skulls) before: plugins that check those causes should check the new ones too, or use `isFireCause()` / `isProjectileCause()`. `CAUSE_MAGIC` keeps its meaning for other magic damage.
- `PlayerDeathEvent::deriveMessage()` returns the new death messages; mob names in its parameters are `Translatable` `entity.<id>.name` keys (named mobs and players keep their name), which `NetworkSession` sends to the client as `%entity.<id>.name`. `Living::getRecentAttacker()` (the mob or player that hurt the entity in the last 5 seconds, also through a projectile or explosion it fired), `PointedDripstone::isStalagmiteTip()`; `KnownTranslationFactory` has the new death messages and the vanilla entity names.
- `WorldCreationOptions::hasBonusChest()` / `setBonusChest()`: a plugin creating a world can give it a bonus chest (off by default, like before).
- `pocketmine\ResourceProfile` (enum `PERFORMANCE` / `ECONOMY`, `fromString()` and the profile's defaults) and `Server::getResourceProfile()`.
